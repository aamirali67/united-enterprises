UNION TEE (3-WAY)

Our heavy-duty high-pressure union tees are engineered to provide reliable, branch-style fluid distribution and line splitting in demanding system layouts. Precision-machined from high-strength 316 stainless steel, these T-shaped fittings offer exceptional resistance to severe vibrations, chemical corrosion, and thermal cycling. They seamlessly connect three lines of high-pressure SS tubing while maintaining an absolute leak-tight mechanical seal at pressures exceeding 10,000 PSI. The optimized internal flow path minimizes turbulence, pressure drop, and erosion at the junction points for maximum system efficiency. Built for rugged industrial environments, these tees ensure quick, safe assembly in critical oil and gas, chemical processing, and hydraulic networks.
