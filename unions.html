STRAIGHT TUBE UNION

Our heavy-duty high-pressure tube unions are engineered to create secure, leak-tight connections in demanding fluid handling systems. Fabricated from premium 316 stainless steel, these fittings ensure exceptional resistance to corrosion, high temperatures, and severe vibrations. Designed to perfectly complement high-pressure SS tubing, they safely maintain system integrity under extreme pressure ratings exceeding 10,000 PSI. The precision-machined threads and ferrules guarantee a reliable, gas-tight mechanical seal without compromising flow efficiency. Upgrade your industrial network with these low-maintenance unions, ideal for critical oil and gas, chemical processing, and hydraulic applications.
