UNION ELBOW (90°)

Our premium high-pressure union elbows are engineered to provide reliable 90-degree directional changes in demanding fluid system layouts. Precision-machined from high-grade 316 stainless steel, these fittings deliver exceptional resistance to mechanical stress, severe vibrations, and harsh chemicals. They seamlessly connect high-pressure SS tubing while maintaining complete leak-tight integrity at extreme operating limits exceeding 10,000 PSI. The engineered internal radius minimizes turbulence and fluid pressure drop, ensuring smooth, unrestricted system flow. Perfect for tight spaces in oil and gas, chemical processing, and hydraulic systems, these rugged elbows guarantee quick, safe installation.
